ABOUT ELDRYN

Melodic House.
Techno.
Breaks.

ELDRYN is a UK electronic music producer creating melodic house, techno and progressive breaks built around emotion, energy and unforgettable dancefloor moments.

Combining cinematic sound design with powerful melodies, driving basslines and modern electronic production, every release is made for clubs, festivals and listeners who want music that feels just as big emotionally as it does on a sound system.

Whether it's an uplifting melodic house anthem, a peak-time techno record or an atmospheric breaks journey, the focus is always the same — creating music people remember long after the final drop.
