About ELDRYN

ELDRYN creates melodic electronic music that blends progressive breaks, melodic house and cinematic sound design. Every track is built around emotion, atmosphere and movement, taking inspiration from sunrise skies, late-night journeys and unforgettable memories.

Rather than following trends, the focus is on timeless melodies, immersive textures and music that stays with the listener long after the final note.
